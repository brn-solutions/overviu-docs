---
title: "Ask Overviu"
description: "Ask how to do something in Overviu and get the steps from these guides, without leaving the app."
---

**Ask Overviu** answers questions about using Overviu, like "How do I connect Stripe?" or "How do guests pay the balance?". Answers come from these guides and end with a link to the guide they're from.

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask Overviu">
    Click **Ask Overviu** in the sidebar, above **Help & guides**. On a phone, tap the sparkles icon at the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Type your question and press Enter, or pick one of the suggested questions. The answer appears as it's written.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up or start over">
    Ask a follow-up question and the assistant remembers what you asked before, even after you move to another page. Click **New question** to start over.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it knows

- It answers from these guides only. If they don't cover your question, it says so and points you here.
- It knows which page you have open and your role, so a member isn't sent to screens only owners and admins can use.
- It doesn't see your team's bookings, guests or money, so ask it how to do things, not about your data.

<Note>Answers can be wrong. If an answer doesn't match what you see, follow the guide it links to.</Note>

## How many questions

Your team can ask 50 questions a day. When they're used up, you can ask again the next day; the guides are always here.
