Everything in Overviu belongs to a team. Invite the people you work with, give each a role, and they see the same listings, calendar, bookings and inbox as you.

## Roles

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listings, calendar, bookings, inbox, messages | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Record a payment taken in person | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Charge and refund guests, security deposits | ✓ | ✓ | |
| API tokens | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Reports and the money on the dashboard | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Move listings to the trash and restore them | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Settings: **Domains**, **Branding**, **Booking site**, **Coupons**, **Payments** | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Rename the team, invite people, cancel invitations | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Change roles and remove members, transfer ownership | ✓ | | |
| Plan and billing | ✓ | | |
| Delete the team | ✓ | | |

Each team has one owner: at first the person who created it, until they [transfer ownership](#transfer-ownership).

## Invite someone

<Note>Only owners and admins can invite people.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    Open the user menu → **Settings** → **Teams**, and open the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite">
    Click **Invite member**. Enter their **Email address**, pick a **Role** (**Admin** or **Member**) and click **Send invitation**. Your plan sets how many people the team can have; see [Plan and billing](/account/plan-and-billing#team-members).
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    They get an email with a link. They sign in, or create an account, with the same email address, and join the team. The invitation expires after 3 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invitations not yet accepted are under **Pending invitations**. **Cancel invitation** withdraws one.

## Change a role or remove someone

<Note>Only the owner can change roles and remove members.</Note>

Under **Team members**, change a person's role, or choose **Remove member**. A removed member loses access to the team straight away; their past work, like notes and recorded payments, stays.

## Work across several teams

You can belong to several teams, for example your own rentals and a client's. Switch with the team switcher at the top of the sidebar: everything you see then belongs to that team.

To start another team, open the team switcher or **Settings** → **Teams**, click **New team**, give it a **Team name** and click **Create team**. You're its owner, and it has its own plan, listings, Stripe account and booking site.

## Rename or delete a team

- **Rename**: change the **Team name** and click **Save**. Renaming changes your booking site's `overviu.app` address; see [Your booking site](/booking-site/set-up#its-address).
- **Delete**: only the owner can **Delete team**, after typing its name to confirm. Its booking site goes down and its plan is cancelled. It can't be undone. The team you got when you signed up can't be deleted here; it goes when you [delete your account](/account/profile-and-security#delete-your-account).

## Transfer ownership

To hand a team to someone else, for example when you sell the business, the owner opens the team in **Settings** → **Teams**, opens a member's role menu under **Team members** and chooses **Transfer ownership**. That person becomes the owner, with the plan and billing, and you stay on as an admin.
