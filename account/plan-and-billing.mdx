Each team has its own plan, paid by the team's owner. Every plan has every feature (the booking site, payments, reports, calendar sync and the API); plans differ in how many units you can list and how many people can be in the team.

<Note>Only the team's owner can choose a plan or manage billing.</Note>

## Plans

| Plan | For | Units | Team members |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Solo** | Small holiday-rental owners getting started | Up to 5 | 1 (just you) |
| **Pro** | Growing property managers | Up to 25 | Up to 10 |

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. The billing page shows the current prices.

### What counts as a unit

- A single property is one unit.
- A building counts its units (the apartments or rooms), not itself.
- Listings in the trash don't count.

When the plan is full, Overviu stops you adding a listing or a unit, and restoring a listing from the trash, with a message to upgrade. See [Create a listing](/listings/create-a-listing#your-plans-limit).

### Team members

Team members count everyone in the team, the owner included, plus invitations still waiting. When the plan is full, **Invite member** says so; an invitation sent earlier can't be accepted either until there's room. See [Team and roles](/account/team-and-roles).

## The free trial

Every new team gets a 14-day free trial, with no unit or member limit and no card needed. Your team's page in **Settings** → **Teams** shows **Free trial** under **Subscription**, and the **Dashboard** reminds you a week before it ends.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    Open the user menu → **Settings** → **Teams**, and open the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe">
    Under **Subscription**, click **Subscribe**. Pick a plan, monthly or yearly, and pay by card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage billing

Under **Subscription**, click **Manage billing** to:

- switch between plans, or between monthly and yearly;
- update your card;
- download your invoices;
- cancel the subscription.

## Without a plan

When a team's trial ends without a plan, or its plan payment fails or the plan is cancelled, it gets a week to sort it out:

1. A week of grace. The team works as usual. The owner gets an email saying when the week ends, and another the day before. **Needs attention** on the **Dashboard** shows the date too.
2. Paused. After the week, the team's pages send everyone to a page saying the team is paused: the owner can choose a plan there, others are asked to contact the owner. The booking site stays online but takes no new bookings, and the API answers `402`.
3. Back on a plan. As soon as the owner subscribes or updates the card, the team works again, exactly as it was left.

Guests who have already booked are looked after as usual while the team is paused: their booking pages, scheduled payments, deposit refunds, automated messages and calendar sync carry on.

<Note>Your plan pays Overviu. It's separate from the guest payments you take through [your own Stripe account](/payments/connect-stripe).</Note>
