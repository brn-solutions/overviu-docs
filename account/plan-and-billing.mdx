Each team has its own plan, paid by the team's owner. Plans differ mainly in how many units you can list.

<Note>Only the team's owner can choose a plan or manage billing.</Note>

## Plans

| Plan | For | Units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Solo** | Small holiday-rental owners getting started | Up to 5 |
| **Pro** | Growing property managers | Up to 25 |

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. The billing page shows the current prices.

### What counts as a unit

- A single property is one unit.
- A building counts its units (the apartments or rooms), not itself.
- Listings in the trash don't count.

When the plan is full, Overviu stops you adding a listing or a unit, and restoring a listing from the trash, with a message to upgrade. See [Create a listing](/listings/create-a-listing#your-plans-limit).

## The free trial

Every new team gets a 14-day free trial, with no unit limit and no card needed. Your team's page in **Settings** → **Teams** shows **Free trial** under **Subscription**, and the **Dashboard** reminds you a week before it ends.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    Open the user menu → **Settings** → **Teams**, and open the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe">
    Under **Subscription**, click **Subscribe**. Pick a plan, monthly or yearly, and pay by card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage billing

Under **Subscription**, click **Manage billing** to:

- switch between plans, or between monthly and yearly;
- update your card;
- download your invoices;
- cancel the subscription.

If a payment for your plan fails, the **Dashboard** tells you under **Needs attention**: update your card to keep using Overviu.

<Note>Your plan pays Overviu. It's separate from the guest payments you take through [your own Stripe account](/payments/connect-stripe).</Note>
