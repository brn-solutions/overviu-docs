Make your booking site look like your business: your colours on buttons and links, your font, and your logo in the header. Guest emails use your logo and colour too.

<Note>Only owners and admins can change the branding.</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open branding">
    Go to **Settings** → **Branding**. The preview at the top shows your booking site's header as you change things.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your colours">
    - **Primary colour**: buttons and highlights, like **Reserve**.
    - **Accent colour** (optional): links and accent text. Leave it empty to use the primary colour.

    Type a hex colour like `#1d4ed8`, or use the picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font">
    Pick a **Font** for the whole site: DM Sans, Inter (the default), Lato, Lora, Merriweather, Montserrat, Nunito, Open Sans, Playfair Display, Poppins, Raleway, Roboto or Work Sans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your logo">
    Under **Logo**, upload a PNG, JPG or WebP of up to 2 MB. It's shown in a circle, so a square image works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where your branding shows

- Your booking site: the colours, font and logo on every page.
- Guest emails: your logo and team name at the top, and buttons in your primary colour. Overviu picks a readable text colour for them.
- Stripe's payment pages use the branding you set in your Stripe account; see [Connect Stripe](/payments/connect-stripe#make-stripes-pages-look-like-yours).

## Start again

**Remove logo** takes the logo off. **Reset to defaults** puts the colours and font back to Overviu's and removes the logo.
