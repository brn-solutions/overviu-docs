When guests reply to an email Overviu sent them (a booking confirmation, an automated message, a reply from you), their reply lands in your **Inbox**, not in someone's personal mailbox. Everyone in the team sees it, with the booking it's about.

## How guests' emails arrive

- Every email Overviu sends a guest has a reply address for that booking. When the guest hits reply, the email arrives in the **Inbox**, filed under the guest and linked to the booking.
- Your team also has its own inbox address. Owners and admins find it in **Settings** → **Teams**, on the team's page, under **Team inbox address**. Forward your own mailbox to it, or publish it: emails sent to it arrive in the **Inbox** too.
- Attachments come with the email: up to 10 files of up to 10 MB each. Larger or extra files are left out, and the message says so.

The **Inbox** item in the sidebar shows how many conversations are unread.

## Reply to a guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Go to **Inbox**. Conversations are in two lists, **Open** and **Done**, newest first. Click one to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it">
    The conversation shows every message between you and the guest, with the booking each one is about. Long quoted replies are folded away: click **Show quoted text** to see them. Download attachments from the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply">
    Write in the **Reply to** box and click **Send reply**. The guest gets it by email, and their answer comes back to the same conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it done">
    When nothing's left to do, click **Done**. It moves to the **Done** list. A new email from the guest reopens it; you can also click **Reopen**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find a conversation

Use **Search guest, email or booking reference** at the top of the **Inbox**, in the **Open** or **Done** list.

## Get notified

To get an email when a guest writes, switch on **Guest message** in your [notification settings](/account/notifications).
