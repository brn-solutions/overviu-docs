---
title: "Send from your own email address"
description: "Send guest emails from an address on your own domain, like bookings@your-domain.com, instead of Overviu's."
---

Guest emails (booking confirmations, automated messages, your replies from the inbox) come from Overviu's address with your team's name. Add your own domain, and they come from an address like `bookings@your-domain.com` instead. Guests recognise it, and it's less likely to land in spam.

<Note>Only owners and admins can add or remove a sender email.</Note>

## Before you start

- A domain you own, like `your-domain.com`, and access to its DNS settings at your domain provider or registrar.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the address">
    Go to **Settings** → **Domains**. Under **Sender email**, type the part before the @ (like `bookings`) and your domain (like `your-domain.com`), and click **Add sender**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Overviu shows the DNS records to add, each with its type, **Name** and **Value**. Copy each one into your domain's DNS settings exactly as shown.

    The record marked **Recommended** (DMARC) isn't needed to verify, but helps your emails reach inboxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the check">
    Overviu checks the records every few minutes. Each record shows **Verified**, **Waiting** or **Not found**. Click **Check now** to check straight away once you've added them; DNS changes can take a while to spread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

When every required record checks out, the sender shows **Verified** and the next guest emails come from your address. Replies still land in your [inbox](/guests/inbox).

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending DNS** | Waiting for the records. Guest emails still come from Overviu's address with your team's name. |
| **Verified** | Guest emails come from your address. |
| **Failed** | The records weren't found within 7 days, or stopped checking out. Guest emails come from Overviu's address again. Fix the records and click **Try again**. |

While the sender is **Pending DNS**, **Change address** changes the part before the @.

## Remove it

Click **Remove** and confirm. Guest emails come from Overviu's address again straight away. You can then remove the DNS records.
