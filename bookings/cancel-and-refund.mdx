---
title: "Cancel a booking"
description: "Cancel a booking, refund what the cancellation policy says, and free its dates."
---

Cancel a booking when the guest isn't coming, whoever cancels. Overviu frees the dates, stops any payments still to come, and suggests a refund from the listing's cancellation policy.

## Before you start

- You can cancel a booking that's **Pending**, **Awaiting payment** or **Confirmed**. **Completed** and **Cancelled** bookings can't be cancelled.
- Anyone in the team can cancel a booking.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking">
    Go to **Bookings** and open the booking. In **Choose an action...** at the top, pick **Cancel booking** and click **Submit**.

    On the **Calendar**, you can also right-click the booking and choose **Cancel booking**. If the guest paid through Stripe, Overviu opens the booking instead, so you can choose the refund; otherwise the booking is cancelled straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    Optionally fill in **Reason (optional)**. It's kept in the booking's **Timeline**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the refund">
    If the guest paid through Stripe, Overviu shows what the listing's policy gives back today, for example "Moderate policy: 50% refund now", and fills in the **Refund** with that amount. Change it if you've agreed something else with the guest: any amount up to what they paid, or 0 to cancel without a refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Click **Cancel booking**. To leave the booking as it is, click **Keep booking**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Stripe refuses the refund, the booking isn't cancelled and Overviu shows Stripe's reason. Fix it, or set the refund to 0 and refund the guest another way.

## What gets refunded

- Overviu refunds only money the guest paid through Stripe. It goes back to the card they paid with, newest payment first.
- Money you [recorded](/bookings/record-a-payment) as cash, bank transfer or card terminal can't be refunded through Stripe. Pay it back the way you received it.
- If Stripe isn't connected, Overviu says "Online payments are not set up, so any refund must be made outside Overviu."
- A security deposit held on the guest's card is returned in full. Payments for extras aren't part of the refund; refund them separately from the booking. See [Refunds](/payments/refunds).

The policies are set per listing; see [Cancellation policy](/calendar/prices-and-rules#cancellation-policy).

## What happens next

- The booking becomes **Cancelled** and its nights are free again, on your calendar, your booking site and the sites you sync with.
- Payments scheduled for later, like a balance due before check-in, won't be taken, and open invoices are voided.
- Your team gets a **Booking cancelled** email, depending on [notification settings](/account/notifications).
- If the listing has an automated message sent **Right after booking cancelled**, the guest gets it. See [Automated messages](/guests/automated-messages).

Bookings that wait for an online payment are also cancelled on their own when the guest doesn't pay in time; your team gets a **Payment not completed** email.
