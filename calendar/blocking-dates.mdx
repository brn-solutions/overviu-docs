---
title: "Block dates"
description: "Close nights you don't want booked, for your own stays, repairs or a break, and open them again."
---

Block a night to stop anyone booking it: when you or your family are staying, during repairs, or when you need a break. Blocked nights are closed on your booking site, and calendar sync passes them on to Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo.

## Block a night

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calendar">
    Open **Calendar** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Block it">
    Shift-click the night on the listing's row, or right-click it and choose **Block day**. For a building, do this on the unit's row.

    In the monthly view, click the day and then **Block**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Blocking works one night at a time: block each night of a longer closure, or close it with a [season](/calendar/prices-and-rules#seasons) that has **Close for arrival** on.

You can't block a night that already has a booking: Overviu says so. Cancel the booking first if you need the night.

## Block a whole building

On the calendar, right-click a night on the building's row and choose **Block whole building**. Overviu blocks that night on every unit. Units that already have a booking that night are left as they are, and Overviu tells you so.

## How blocked nights look

- A red ✕ is a night you blocked.
- A night blocked by calendar sync shows the site's name instead, like "Airbnb". Those come from the other site: free them there, not in Overviu. If you unblock one here, it comes back on the next sync.
- In the monthly view, a blocked day shows **Blocked**.

## Unblock a night

Click the blocked night on the calendar, or right-click it and choose **Unblock day**. In the monthly view, click the day and then **Unblock**.

## Blocked nights in reports

Your own blocked nights don't count as available in [reports](/insights/reports), so blocking a week for repairs doesn't lower your occupancy. Nights blocked by calendar sync count as booked, because they're usually bookings on the other site.
