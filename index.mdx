Overviu is a property management platform for holiday rentals. It keeps your listings, calendar, bookings, guests and money in one place, and gives you a booking site of your own so guests can book you directly.

## What you can do with Overviu

- Listings: single properties, or buildings with apartments or rooms let separately, each with photos, amenities and a public listing page.
- Calendar and pricing: nightly prices, seasons, minimum stays and blocked dates, kept in step with Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo through calendar sync.
- Bookings: one list for every booking, wherever it came from, with its guest, payments and messages.
- Payments: guests pay through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you. Overviu handles part payments, balances, security deposits, refunds and coupons.
- Your booking site: a direct booking site with your colours and logo, on an overviu.app address or your own domain.
- Guests: a booking page for every guest (ID check, rental agreement, deposit, house information), automated messages and one inbox for replies.
- Insights: a dashboard for the day ahead and reports on occupancy, revenue and money.

Overviu is for hosts and small teams who run one or more holiday rentals: owners, property managers and their cleaners or co-hosts.
